Framework Overview & Mathematical Formulation

The SENTINEL prototype predicts cash-withdrawal hot zones in advance by correlating geospatial proximity, sliding temporal cybercrime complaint density, forced withdrawal flags, banking ledger cash flow anomalies, and repeat suspect telecom footprints.

Hotspot Risk Scoring Formula (v2.0)

RiskScore(a) = [ w₂₄ · C(a, W₂₄) + w₄₈ · C(a, W₄₈) + w₇₂ · C(a, W₇₂) ] + 1.5 · C_withdrawal + 1.0 · C_repeat_suspect + 2.0 · TX_fraud + 1.5 · TX_mule_phone

  • w₂₄ = 1.0, w₄₈ = 0.6, w₇₂ = 0.3: Temporal decay weights prioritizing recent velocity.
  • C(a, W): Count of cyber complaints within radius $R = 3.0\text{ km}$ calculated via Great-Circle Haversine distance.
  • C_withdrawal (Weight 1.5): Additional gravity factor when prior complaints resulted in forced mule account withdrawals at/near the ATM.
  • C_repeat_suspect (Weight 1.0): Repeat suspect bonus when distinct victims report identical anonymized phone_hash rings.
  • TX_fraud & TX_mule_phone (Banking Corroboration): Extra weight when ATM terminal ledger logs confirmed fraudulent withdrawals or transactions from suspect phone hashes.

Privacy & Anonymization Standard

All sensitive information is masked or hashed:

  • Suspect phone numbers are hashed using irreversible cryptographic digests (phone_hash).
  • Victim bank accounts are masked to tokenized references (e.g. AC-314650).
  • No personal identifiable information (PII) is stored or exposed.

Proactive LEA Dispatch Protocol

When an ATM exceeds Risk Score ≥ 7.0:

  • Local Police Patrol units are alerted to conduct physical checks and deter mule withdrawals.
  • Bank Vigilance teams receive instant alerts to flag multi-card rapid-sequence ATM withdrawals and verify CCTV feeds.
